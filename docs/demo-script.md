# Demo script (about 90 seconds)

Record on a real device, outdoors where possible. Show true results, including a slow response if it happens.

| Time | Show | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | A balcony or garden spot | "Beginners don't know what to grow or how to look after it." |
| 0:10 | Garden page: fill in the profile | "I tell GrowMate where I garden, how much sun I get, and my space." |
| 0:25 | Generate a planting plan | "Gemma runs on my laptop through Ollama. No cloud model." Show the AI status pill. |
| 0:45 | Add one recommendation with its tasks | "I choose what to add. Nothing is saved without my say-so." |
| 0:55 | Go outside, do the task | Film the real action (watering, checking soil). |
| 1:10 | Complete the task, write a journal entry | "A real observation goes into the growth journal." |
| 1:20 | Stop Ollama, ask a question | "If the model is down, the app says what to do and everything else keeps working." |
| 1:30 | Close | "Open-weight AI, running locally, that sends you outside." |

Do not say the app is "fully offline" if you used weather or research during the demo.
